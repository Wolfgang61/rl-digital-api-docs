# Welcome to Electronic Product Leaflet

The EPL (Electronic Product Leaflet, formerly the “EPL Pilot Project”; the “GI” in “diGItal” stands for “Gebrauchsinformation” / package leaflet) project to introduce digital package inserts (GI) in Germany is being carried out and initiated by the manufacturers’ associations BPI, Pharma Deutschland, Pro Generika, and vfa.

Alternative names: Digital, diGital, Digitalpilot, DIGI, DIG, DI, ...

Project Objective
The main motivation behind this project is to promote the digitization of patient information and to establish a unified platform. The pilot project aims to demonstrate that the German pharmaceutical industry is a pioneer in this field.

Project Context
The project is sponsored by PharmaProtect Solutions GmbH (abbreviated “PPS,” formerly “ACS PharmaProtect GmbH,” abbreviated “ACS”). The company develops and operates the National Medicines Verification System (NMVS) database on behalf of securPharm e.V. to document the legality of supply chains for prescription drugs. ACS has contracts with all companies that offer prescription drugs.
